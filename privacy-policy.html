Personal data we collect

Accounts and contact details

Name, email address, telephone number, account credentials and communications with our team.

Event and participant information

Studio and team details, dancer names, dates of birth, age groups, categories, performance information, music uploads, accreditation and results. Where a participant is a minor, information must be provided by an authorised adult or studio representative.

Transactions and tickets

Order, invoice, payment status and ticket information. Payment card details are handled by the relevant payment provider and are not intended to be stored by Space4Dance.

Photos, video and event media

Images, recordings and event coverage created at competitions, where permitted by the event rules, notices and applicable law.

Technical information

Device, browser, approximate location, log and website interaction data. Optional analytics is collected only after consent, as explained in our Cookie Policy.

Why and how we use data

Contract

To create accounts, process applications and tickets, organise participation and provide requested event services.

Legal obligation

To meet accounting, tax, consumer protection and lawful authority requirements.

Legitimate interests

To operate secure events, prevent misuse, answer enquiries, improve services and document or promote our events where those interests do not override individual rights.

Consent

For optional analytics, direct marketing where used, and other processing for which consent is specifically requested. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

We do not use personal data for decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Who may receive data

Data may be shared only as necessary with service providers supporting hosting, accounts, ticketing, payments, communications and event operations; venues and authorised event partners; professional advisers; and public authorities where required by law.

If a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, we use the safeguards required by applicable data-protection law. Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy notices.

How long we keep data

We retain data only for as long as required for the purpose for which it was collected, including the duration of an account or event relationship, statutory accounting and limitation periods, dispute handling and legitimate event archives. Consent records are retained as described in the Cookie Policy. When information is no longer required, it is deleted or anonymised where reasonably possible.

Your data rights

Subject to the GDPR and applicable exceptions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection to processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Contact us at info@space4dance.org. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may also lodge a complaint with Latvia’s Data State Inspectorate, dvi.gov.lv.

Security and updates

We use organisational and technical measures intended to protect personal data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a strong password and contact us if you suspect misuse of your account.

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The current version and update date will remain available on this page.
