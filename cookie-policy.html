About this policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026.

This policy explains how Space4Dance, operated by TK EVENTS SIA, uses cookies and similar browser storage on this website. A cookie is a small text file stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website.

Cookie categories

Necessary

These technologies support essential functions such as security, account sessions and remembering your privacy choice. They cannot be disabled through our consent panel.

Analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics helps us understand visits, devices, approximate locations and interactions so we can improve the website.

Marketing

This category is reserved for advertising and campaign measurement services. No marketing service is currently activated through the consent manager.

Cookies and storage we use

Name Provider Purpose Duration
s4d_cookie_consent Space4Dance Local browser storage recording your cookie preferences and the policy version. 6 months, or until browser data is cleared or the policy version changes.
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes visitors for website analytics. Set only after analytics consent. Up to 2 years, subject to browser limits.
_ga_<container-id> Google Analytics Maintains analytics session state. Set only after analytics consent. Up to 2 years, subject to browser limits.

Your choices

On your first visit, optional analytics and marketing technologies remain disabled. You may accept all, reject optional technologies or choose categories in Settings.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time. Use the button below; withdrawing analytics consent removes accessible Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop further analytics collection.

Contact

For questions about cookies or personal data, contact TK EVENTS SIA / Space4Dance at info@space4dance.org.

This policy should be reviewed whenever the website adds or changes analytics, advertising, embedded media or other third-party services.
