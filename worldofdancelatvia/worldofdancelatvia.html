Advancing divisions

Three paths to the World Finals

Every dancer is placed by age and experience. These three Championship Series divisions qualify for the World of Dance Finals.

12 & UNDER

Youth Team Division

Ages 12 and under* Group size 5 or more

Experience & background

  • Dancers have performance experience, though they may be newer to the community and industry setting.
  • Dancers demonstrate a developing and consistent understanding of formations and stage use.
  • Choreography complexity varies according to age and group level.
Eligibility details

*Youth Team Division Eligibility: All dancers must be 12 years of age or younger as of January 1st of the competition year. Dancers who begin the season at age 12 and celebrate their 13th birthday later in the year may remain in the Youth Division through the conclusion of that competition year, including finals.

Teams composed of dancers under 13 may elect to participate in the Junior Team Division. This must apply to the entire team.

13–17

Junior Team Division

Ages 13–17** Group size 5 or more

Experience & background

  • Dancers have performance experience in a pre-professional, community, or industry competition setting.
  • Dancers demonstrate a consistent and efficient understanding of formations and stage use.
  • Choreography reflects maturity, precision, and sustained complexity throughout the set.
Eligibility details

**Junior Team Division Eligibility: All dancers must be 17 years of age or younger as of January 1st of the competition year. Dancers who begin the season at age 17 and later turn 18 may continue with their team through finals.

Currently enrolled high school students who are 18+ on January 1st may contact World of Dance for eligibility.

ALL AGES

Team Division

Ages All ages Group size 5 or more

Experience & background

  • Dancers have performance experience in a pre-professional, community, or industry competition setting.
  • Dancers exhibit an efficient understanding of intricate formations and use of audition space.
  • The complexity of choreography, originality, and exceptional creativity are demonstrated throughout the set.
